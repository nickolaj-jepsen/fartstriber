# Taste table

What the owner has cared about more than once, as checks. Builders check every row before a round; the critic passes or fails every row in every round verdict (`taste`), and a design ships only when all of them pass (`livery critic status`). Their words are quoted from `prefs/log.md` and `prefs/decisions.jsonl`; where a row comes from the critic's recurring `lint_missed` instead, it says so.

| id | Their words | Look for | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| `boring` | "they are just too boring" (18 Oct, fireproof-sweep and -blocks); "the design is a bit boring" (18 Oct, eva-unit01); "Better, but still a bit boring" (19 Oct, eva-nerv) | Every view has a second layer at 10 m (tones of a field's hue, pinlines, a dissolve, stripe bundles, linework), not only the big shape. No plain field larger than a door on a side, or than the bonnet on top. No view plainer than the brief's favourites, none as plain as the boring anchors. | Every shot and its distance image, beside the favourite and boring anchors |
| `front-as-back` | "The back design is nice, but the front is a bit boring"; "I like the pixel design a lot, but it's totally missing from the front" (19 Oct, fireproof-v2) | The front carries the same system as the sides and tail, as layered: bonnet, bumper, splitter and front fenders, not a plain field or another idea. | front34, top (bonnet), nose crop, against rear34 |
| `stroke-between-colours` | "it would look a lot better if there where some kind of "stroke" between the colors?" (19 Oct, eva-unit01) | Where two fields meet, the edge is an event (a pinline, a dissolve, a raked cut) all along it, even in width, and visible in the shots, not lost to thinness or low contrast. | side_l, side_r, beltline and nose crops |
| `type-designed` | "The side text is a bit boring" (19 Oct, eva-unit01); "Here the alignment/spacing on the text is off" (19 Oct, eva-nerv) | One type system per field: a designation with small lines aligned to it, a shared left edge or baseline, even line gaps, padding and tracking, one display face used decisively. Not a stack of labels. | Full side shots, top, every crop with text |
| `ai-vibe-letters` | "There's a weird "ai generated/diffusion model" vibe to these" (19 Oct, Altmere); "again, a bit AI like" (19 Oct, Ostrand) | Letters and marks keep a font's own forms or clean geometry. Fails on soft or melted joins, rounded-off corners, filled counters, or letters that change width or weight from one to the next. | Full shots and crops, every wordmark and line of text |
| `brand-every-view` | "just remove the wordmark (or keep it smaller/more descrete), it's not imporant for my own personal brand" (19 Oct, fireproof-v2): the brief decides | Where the brief asks for a lead brand, it reads from the side, from the front (bonnet or nose) and from behind (rear deck or bumper). A brief that asks for none, or for it small, passes when the car follows it. | side_l, side_r, front34, rear34, top |
| `nothing-cut` | The critic's `lint_missed` in every design: "reads as 'YO-3'" (eva-nerv), "reads 'PE-01'" under the sim's light panel (eva-unit01), a sill sponsor "shows as fragments" under the side blade (fireproof-v2) | No text, mark or number is cut, bent or hidden by a wheel arch, the side blade, a crease, a shut line, the sim's light panel or its number. Every word reads whole wherever it shows. | Full side shots, front34, rear34, crops |
| `seam-slant-step` | "Theres a wierd "slant" to the side-line" (18 Oct, fireproof-stripes); "the black horizontal line is still not perfectly algined", "see the differences on the wheel arches" (18 Oct, the kit) | A line drawn straight runs straight and level: no slant, step or doubling at a seam or shut line, the same height either side of an arch and on both sides. A failure is a kit fault: it goes under `blocked`, for the owner, not into a fix. | side_l, side_r, beltline crop, top |
| `stray-lines` | "there are more lines on the bonnet that then texture seems to apply" (18 Oct, fireproof-stripes) | No line the design doesn't draw: no light hairlines along seams or panel edges, no base colour showing at a shut line or round lights and vents. A failure is a renderer or kit fault: under `blocked`. | Nose crop, front34, top |
