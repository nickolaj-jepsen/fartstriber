# fireproof-v3b notes

Build stage, refinement round 3, a quick fix on the owner's note only: he liked the concept but not the execution, and not the cyan (21 Oct), and let the builder try yellow, paper, purple and magenta side by side and pick. The contrast is yellow #D0A215 (see Contrast). Round 2's critic passed the front and the roof's framed panels but saw the doors and haunch as thick terracotta maze strokes with empty insides, and the rear-bumper column's lower mark cut by the bumper's edge. This round the sides have their own finer pixel and are cut to fit the visible side, so each door and haunch panel is whole and holds a frame, a ring and a core pattern; the tail's lower body rises as a wedge and the column sits higher; the duct group is headed by a Drexa wordmark.

## The design

- **Base:** #100F0F gloss, with a tone-on-tone pixel weave in #1C1B1A over every view (a two-cell tile in texture space, drawn with `svg`) for the 1 m read. The weave and the texture-space tiles stop out of the near-tangent lint's reach of every paint edge, shut line and number zone (`geom.edges` with the library's margins) and off the number zones.
- **Contrast:** `contrast` 0, yellow #D0A215 (`CONTRASTS`: yellow, bright paper #F2F0E5, purple #8B7EC8, magenta #CE5D97; no cyan). It shows on the splitter lip, mirror shells, wing endplates, the windows' corner brackets and the nose and tail panels' inner lines. Picked from `livery sheet fireproof-v3b --wedge contrast=0..3..1` on the current build, the four colours side by side in the UI car view. Yellow has the strongest step off the warm black at distance, and as a warm ember beside the terracotta it ties to the Fireproof flame. Bright paper blends with the #DAD8CE sponsor marks and reads as unpainted trim. Purple contrasts in hue but sinks into the black at distance. Magenta sits too close to the terracotta to read as a second colour. Sim run 20261022-021133 confirms it: the wing endplates, mirrors, lip and brackets read in every shot.
- **Edges:** every panel colour, the lower body, the windows, plates and brackets are projected per view and snapped with `geom.snapped` (23 moves, listed in the check report). Where no snap clears a stretch, the pixels within reach of that stretch of line are left out.
- **Panels, top:** the top view is cut into raked polygons by recursive splits in the view as seen. A cut is raked (`lean` 28°, outer ends sweeping back), parallel to the band, or corner to corner. A piece stops splitting once its area is under `small` 0.025 + (`big` 0.06 - `small`) × band-heat^1.5 u² as seen. `seed` 3: seeds 1-6 side by side, 3 had the clearest separate panels on the roof (seed 2 crashes the library's snapping, see Constraints). Cell `cell` 0.0085 u (≈13 texture px).
- **Panels, sides:** their own pixel, `side_cell` 0.0065 u (≈10 px): at the top's cell the side field between the lower body and the window line was about 13 cells tall, so a 2-cell frame and 2-cell ring left the core a line or nothing, which read as a maze of thick strokes (round 2's first fix). The split region is the side's visible paint (`_skin`: where both side maps land, sampled through the kit's pieces, plus the front fender ahead of its rear edge less the arch), so panels end whole at the window, arches and shoulder instead of as frames cut in half. Side pieces are cut only across the field (`along_min`), never one narrower than 2 × `side_min_w` 8 cells, and stop under `side_small` 0.011 + (`side_big` 0.022 - `side_small`) × heat^1.5 u², so the door behind the number window and the haunch ahead of the light panel are each one or two whole panels. The door's rear shut line is a gutter (a panel across it would run its stair edges along the line); its front edge (`front_seam` 0) is not, since a gutter there left the fender wedge in slivers. `side_gap` 2 cells between panels and `side_inset` 0 against the paint edges (at 3 and 0.5 the sides were over half black). `side_seed` 5: seeds 0-9 side by side, 5 has a solid door panel with cut linework, a solid haunch panel with a stepped zigzag core and a dim fender triangle, and raked dash strips over the haunch top and behind the rear arch.
- **Heat:** on the sides, a band whose centre climbs from v `band_v0` 0.44 at the nose by `band_dv` 0.46 to the tail, half width `band_w` 0.17 v. On top, centred at u `top_u` 0.47, sweeping back `top_sweep` 0.35 u per w, half width `top_w` 0.22 u. On both, the nose has its own heat, `nose_heat` 1.0 falling off over `nose_w` 0.4 u. Strength by heat: ≥ `solid` 0.75 solid (on the sides only `side_solid_share` 0.55 of those, so hot panels with terracotta linework on #6B3528 sit among them), ≥ `hot` 0.55 hot, below it dim.
- **Rasterising** (`_rasterise`, with a `Nest` per view saying how deep a panel must be for each layer). Three strengths of one system:
  - solid: a terracotta frame, a black ring, then solid terracotta with black linework cut in (hatch, saw, diamond or chevron);
  - hot: a terracotta frame, a black ring, on deep enough panels a terracotta border row (rope, ladder, teeth) and another black ring, then a #6B3528 ground with terracotta linework (any of the six core patterns);
  - dim: a #6B3528 frame, a black ring, then #6B3528 with black linework.
  Top: frame 2 cells from 6 deep, 1 from `framed` 5, ring 2 cells from `ring2` 6, gutter `gap` 3 cells. Sides: frame 2 cells from 7 deep, 1 from 4, ring always 1 cell, so the core keeps room for its pattern; panels under 12 cells are left out (24 on top). Nose and tail panels (side u < 0.3 or > 0.85, top u < 0.25 or > 0.88) carry a yellow inner line inside the ring (`accent_share` 1.0). Gutter, frame and linework are the three line weights. Every stair climbs towards the tail.
- **Solid fields:** the lower body, plain #100F0F under v `sill_v` 0.24 on the door, then `crease_pad` 0.06 v above the intake crease (`intake_crease_*`), then rising behind the rear arch to v `tail_v` 0.74 at the tail as a wedge (round 2 fell back to 0.40, too low for the rear-bumper column); the number windows (number_l, number_r, number_top grown by `window_pad` 1 cell, raked `window_lean` 14° on the sides), plain black with stepped yellow corner brackets (`bracket` 4.5 cells per arm). The sim's light panel (`door_light_panel_*`) sits in a window of the same kind with the same brackets. Two black plates on the bonnet's front corners (top view, u `plate_u` 0.05 ± `plate_du` 0.025, w ±`plate_w` 0.3 ± `plate_dw` 0.12) hold the nose marks.
- **Hardware:** intake strakes terracotta; splitter lip, mirror shells and wing endplates yellow. The bonnet vent (`bonnet_vent`), both front bumper corners and the rear face group: a texture-space terracotta stair tile at the top's cell size, two cells on and two off (no view maps them).
- **Sponsors**, all in #DAD8CE on black, 23 in all: per side, Grivant, Huilec, Telvio marks on the lower door (u 0.42-`mark_u1` 0.49, `mark_v` 0.14); Ostrand, Rigmount, Hexmoor marks on the duct behind the door (u 0.668-0.765, v 0.13) headed by a Drexa wordmark (u `duct_u2` 0.69-0.765, `duct_v2` 0.28), the two rows sharing their right edge (the optional fix: a wordmark among the icon marks); Norvane and Altmere marks in a column on the rear bumper behind the wheel (u 0.977, v `tail_v0` 0.26-`tail_v1` 0.51, wholly on the bumper's visible face). On the bonnet plates, Brandvik and Hexmoor marks on each side. No Fireproof mark.
- **Not yet drawn from the brief:** race markings, the optional flame mark.

## Constraints that shaped the layout

- The side field: the number zones (`number_l`/`_r`, v 0.288-0.53 at u 0.408-0.471) and the sim's light panel (`door_light_panel_*`, v 0.45-0.7 at u 0.712-0.772) fill most of the height between the lower body and the window line (v 0.83 on the door), so the panels above them are strips 4-5 cells deep; only the door behind the number window, the haunch ahead of the light panel and the fender wedge behind the front arch hold full nesting.
- The side maps barely land on the front fender ahead of u 0.285 (sampled through the kit's pieces: thin strips); the fender's side is seen through the top view's maps, so `_skin` takes all of it there less the arch.
- The door's front edge (`door_front_edge_*`, the i158/i128 and i054/i046 boundary) slants against the lean from (0.404, 0.225) to (0.33, 0.56); a gutter along it split the fender wedge into slivers, so panels cross it.
- The door sill field (u 0.415-0.51, before the carbon side blade at u 0.52) is 0.095 car lengths long: three roster lockups need at least 0.0154-0.0171 car lengths of height each and don't fit; only the marks do.
- The `crease` lint on the door sill row (front34/rear34 see a fold there): at v 0.12 Telvio and Huilec straddle it, at v 0.08 Grivant and Huilec, at v 0.14-0.16 Telvio when the row runs to u 0.51; v 0.18 crowds the number window. So the row sits at v 0.14 and ends at u 0.49.
- The number zones: a window grown one cell runs inside the lint's 12 px reach of the zone; `geom.snapped` moves its edges 3-9 px clear.
- The intake duct below the crease (u 0.65-0.776): the mark row sits at v 0.13; the Drexa wordmark needs 0.0135 car lengths of height (20 px for its strokes), which a field from u 0.705 (0.06 long) didn't give, so it starts at u 0.69, at v 0.28 under the crease (v 0.33 there).
- The rear bumper side behind the rear arch (u 0.952-1.0): the side maps land only above v 0.23 there, so round 2's column at v 0.15-0.42 lost its lower mark off the bumper's face; the two marks need 0.043 car lengths (Norvane 0.0186, Altmere 0.0189 at their minimum), v 0.26-0.51, so the lower body rises to v 0.74 at the tail to stay under them.
- The bonnet's front corners: a plate at w ±0.36 put the marks off every mapped island; at w ±0.3 they sit inside `series_bonnet_top` (|w| < 0.32), a series-sponsor zone the owner's races don't stamp (lint warning only).
- side_l/side_r never see u 0.286-0.402 below v 0.54 (i128/i046 behind the front wheel, `unseen_side_*`): no sponsors there. The side_l preview camera puts the sill below the grass line, so the door-sill marks show only in side_r, front34 and rear34.
- The front bumper corners (i021, i020), the rear face (i003), valance (i017) and the bonnet vent (i004, i058, i152, i107) are on no view, so they get a texture-space tile. Only the vent's ramp (i004) is seen (lint `hidden_detail`: 86%). `sponsor` needs a view anchor, so the optional flame mark on the rear face can't be placed with it.
- `pattern_fill` has no way to keep a tile out of the lint's reach of the lines, so the tiles are drawn with `svg` over the region less `geom.edges(...).near` and the zones. A `pattern_fill(..., clear=True)` would be the library version.
- The library has no car-space outline of what a view's maps show; `_skin` samples `carspace.pieces` itself. A `carspace.seen(kit, view)` would be the library version.
- The lint's `screen_width` warns that the weave tile is at most 5.1 px on screen: it is the 1 m tone-on-tone layer.
- `seed` 2 crashes the library: `geom.snapped` → `Edges.alongside` gets `None` back from `edge.difference(self.meet)` (`AttributeError: 'NoneType' object has no attribute 'intersection'`, livery/geom.py:260) on one of that layout's panel shapes.
- Baked views are not usable for this kit (`livery bake status`: the capture 20261020-100748 predates changes to the sim window size, camera set and graphics options; its bake check failed, worst p95 60.0), so colours were chosen on flat views and confirmed in the sim.
