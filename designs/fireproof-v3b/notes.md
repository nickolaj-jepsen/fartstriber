# fireproof-v3b notes

Build stage: ranked 1 of 6 concepts; the concept is kept as sketched, made to pass `livery check`, built and shot in the sim (no critic fixes yet). Open risk the pair critics all named: the raked bright band barely reads at distance, so the pattern can look like an even print; and the cyan-framed number windows read heavy, like UI windows.

## The design

- **Base:** #100F0F gloss, with a tone-on-tone pixel weave in #1C1B1A over every view (a two-cell tile in texture space, drawn with `svg`) for the 1 m read. The weave and the texture-space tiles stop out of the near-tangent lint's reach of every paint edge, shut line and number zone (`geom.edges` with the library's margins) and off the number zones, so their pixels run on across seams and stop clear of every other line.
- **Edges:** every panel colour, the lower body, the number windows and keylines are projected per view and snapped with `geom.snapped` (15 moves, listed in the check report: the window and keyline edges 4-8 px clear of the number zones, the lower body's foot at u 0.52 and u 0.67-0.68 on both sides, a few panel edges at the nose and on top). Where no snap clears a stretch (two on top, texture (365, 168) by a shut line and (608, 292) by a paint edge), the pixels within reach of that stretch of line are left out.
- **Panels:** each view (side_l, side_r, top) is cut into raked polygons by recursive splits in the view as seen (v or w scaled by the view's aspect so a cell is square). A cut is raked (`lean` 28°, tops towards the tail; on top the outer ends sweep back), parallel to the band, or corner to corner (stepped triangles). A piece stops splitting once its area is under `small` + (`big` - `small`) × heat^1.5 (`big` 0.03, `small` 0.008 u² as seen). Both sides draw from one stream (`split-r`, `cells-r`), so the sides carry the same layout.
- **Heat:** on the sides, a band whose centre climbs from v `band_v0` 0.44 at the nose by `band_dv` 0.46 to the tail, half width `band_w` 0.17 v: low on the front fender, over the door top and the haunch. On top, centred at u `top_u` 0.47 (the roof), sweeping back `top_sweep` 0.35 u per w at the outer edges, half width `top_w` 0.14 u. Heat ≥ `hot` 0.55 is terracotta #CF6A4C, below it #6B3528; heat ≥ `solid` 0.75 is solid terracotta with a finer #6B3528 stair hatch on half cells (`solid_share` 1.0). The solid panels make the bright raked diagonal at 50 m.
- **Rasterising:** every panel is drawn on one pixel grid (`cell` 0.0085 u ≈ 13 texture px), shrunk by `gap` 1.2 cells for the black between panels: a one-cell stepped outline in its own ink, a one-cell black ring, then one pattern: staircase hatching, stair sawtooth, concentric stepped diamonds, checker dither, rows of chevrons pointing back, or a grid of 2×2 embers. Every stair climbs towards the tail. Nose (u < 0.16 on the sides, < 0.12 on top) and tail (u > 0.9, > 0.92) panels get a cyan #3AA99F outline half the time (`cyan_share`).
- **Solid fields:** the lower body, plain #100F0F under v `sill_v` 0.24 on the door, then `crease_pad` 0.06 v above the intake crease, so it rises to the tail as a wedge; the number windows (number_l, number_r, number_top grown by `window_pad` 1 cell, raked `window_lean` 14° on the sides), plain black with a one-cell stepped cyan keyline.
- **Hardware:** intake strakes terracotta; splitter lip, mirror shells and wing endplates cyan. Rear face group and both front bumper corners: a texture-space #6B3528 stair tile (no view maps them).
- **Sponsors:** #DAD8CE on the black lower body, per side: Grivant, Huilec and Telvio marks on the lower door (u 0.42-`mark_u1` 0.49, `mark_v` 0.14); Ostrand, Rigmount and Hexmoor marks on the intake duct (u 0.668-0.765, v 0.13). Six per side, twelve in all. No Fireproof mark yet.
- **Not yet drawn from the brief:** Space Mono tech labels in #878580, race markings, sponsors on the nose corners and rear bumper, the optional flame mark.

## Constraints that shaped the layout

- The door sill field (u 0.415-0.51, before the carbon side blade at u 0.52) is 0.095 car lengths long: three roster lockups need at least 0.0154-0.0171 car lengths of height each and don't fit; only the marks do.
- The `crease` lint on the door sill row (front34/rear34 see a fold there): at v 0.12 Telvio and Huilec straddle it (27-35%), at v 0.08 Grivant and Huilec, at v 0.14-0.16 Telvio when the row runs to u 0.51; v 0.18 clears it but crowds the number keyline. So the row sits at v 0.14 and ends at u 0.49, tightly packed.
- The number zones (`number_l`/`_r`/`_top`): a window grown one cell (≈13 px) runs inside the lint's 12 px reach of the zone; `geom.snapped` moves its edges 4-8 px clear.
- The intake duct below the crease (u 0.65-0.776) gives a 0.041 car-length column at v 0.07-0.29: three marks don't stack there (Ostrand's mark needs 0.0217); they sit in a row instead.
- side_l/side_r never see u 0.286-0.402 below v 0.54 (i128/i046 behind the front wheel, `unseen_side_*`): no sponsors there, so the sill row starts at u 0.42.
- The sim's door light panel (`door_light_panel_*`, u 0.712-0.772, v 0.45-0.7) is left as a black hole in the haunch panels.
- The front bumper corners (i021, i020), the rear face (i003) and valance (i017) are on no side view, so they get a texture-space tile, not car-space panels; `sponsor` needs a view anchor, so the optional flame mark on the rear face can't be placed with it.
- `pattern_fill` has no way to keep a tile out of the lint's reach of the lines (the weave failed near-tangent at the door foot, the top's edge and the rear-face number zone), so the tiles are drawn with `svg` over the region less `geom.edges(...).near` and the zones. A `pattern_fill(..., clear=True)` would be the library version.
- Baked views are not usable for this kit yet (`livery bake status`: bake check failed, worst p95 60.0).
