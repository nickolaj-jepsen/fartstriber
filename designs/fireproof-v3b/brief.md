# Fireproof v3b: 8-bit linework

Nickolaj, 21 Oct 2026, in his words: "Can you help me do another take on the fireproof livery, i really like the pixel motif, but the rest i'm not really sold on, and i think a couple of factors fed into it not as good as i've hoped: 1. it was one of the first prototypes, before we've nailed down the workflow and preview tools 2. I was lazy with the feedback 3. Irrelevant \"requirements\" from the design skill, like having a huge focus on the wordmark (which would be relevant for a real sponsor, but for my own brand, i'd rather it just being cool-looking). So can you help me do another similar design, please avoid grounding it too much in the initial design, and use the color scheme from this file: github.com/nickolaj-jepsen/nixos modules/base/theme.nix"

What holds:

- A new Fireproof car on the kit mclaren-720s-gt3-evo, not a revision of fireproof-v2 or fireproof-stripes: keep the pixel motif (hard square pixels, stepped blocks, pixel dissolves, embers), drop their layout (orange car, yellow band nose to tail, yellow spine, black tail wedge, flame tongues). Built from this brief, not from those designs' code.
- Palette: his Fireproof desktop theme (Flexoki-based), not the pixel flame's #f46200/#ff8000/#ffd500. Neutrals: black #100F0F, bg #1C1B1A, bgAlt #282726, ui #343331, uiAlt #403E3C, muted #878580, fgAlt #B7B5AC, paper #DAD8CE, bright paper #F2F0E5. Accent #CF6A4C (terracotta) with its dark container #6B3528. Other hues, each with a darker alt: red #D14D41/#AF3029, orange #DA702C/#BC5215, yellow #D0A215/#AD8301, green #879A39/#66800B, cyan #3AA99F/#24837B, blue #4385BE/#205EA6, purple #8B7EC8/#5E409D, magenta #CE5D97/#A02F6F. The identity is the warm dark greys, the paper and the terracotta; at most three hue families. Hex values as given.
- Cool-looking first. No wordmark-led layout and no big type: Fireproof is optional, at most one small discreet mark in one palette colour (he disliked the pixel wordmark on the car, 19 Oct). The design leads. Small roster sponsors as the raced-car ecosystem, one colour per field.
- Number zones clear, on one solid field with contrast for the white number; edges clearly cross or avoid seams and shut lines.

- 21 Oct, at the concept pick: "I like the concept, but not the execution, and not really the cyan either, i get there has to be a \"contrast\" color, but maybe try another?" Asked which, he chose to let the builder try a few from the theme side by side (yellow #D0A215, paper #F2F0E5, purple #8B7EC8, magenta #CE5D97) and pick. No cyan.

## The concept

- **Source:** Favourite 34, SPIRIT Dallara P217: a dark base covered in panels of geometric linework (triangles, zigzags, hatching) in warm inks, panels of very different sizes over every surface, nested frames and border rows inside each panel, accent mirrors. Here every line is drawn as pixels: 8-bit stair patterns in terracotta on warm black, with Flexoki yellow #D0A215 as the contrast (chosen from yellow, paper, purple and magenta side by side: the strongest step off the black, and the ember tone of the Fireproof flame).
- **Expected (banned):** a black car with terracotta squares sprinkled evenly over it plus a terracotta stripe (the pixel motif as wallpaper). Nor a straight SPIRIT copy: smooth diagonal linework that isn't pixel at all, laid out evenly with no direction.
- **The move:** every painted surface is a panel of 8-bit linework. The only solid colour is the black between the panels, the number windows, the sills and the yellow accents.

## Three distances

- **50 m:** a warm black car glowing terracotta all over, brightest along one raked diagonal. Base #100F0F about 45% (the gutters, the lower body, the windows); terracotta #CF6A4C about 35%, #6B3528 about 15%; yellow #D0A215 under 10% (mirror shells, splitter lip, wing endplates, the windows' corner brackets, inner lines of the nose and tail panels). The panels are largest and solid terracotta along a band raked with the lean: low on the front fender, up across the top of the door and the haunch, over the roof as a chevron sweeping back; the nose is as hot as the band, so the front is as strong as the back. Away from the band, towards the lower tail and the outer engine cover, they get smaller and dim (#6B3528), so the pattern has one direction and is not an even print.
- **10 m:** raked polygons (parallelograms, trapezoids, stepped triangles) from a quarter door to a whole door in size, each a filled panel with a black gutter round it, in SPIRIT's nested frames as pixels and in three strengths of the same system: solid terracotta with black linework cut in (the band's heart and the nose), terracotta linework on a #6B3528 ground (the band's edges), and #6B3528 with black linework (far from the band). Each framed panel: a stepped frame, a black ring, on the larger hot ones a three-cell border row (stair rope, ladder or stepped teeth), and a core pattern (staircase hatching, stair sawtooth, stepped diamonds, staggered dots, pixel chevrons pointing back, ember squares); panels too thin for a frame are toned blocks with linework. Gutter, frame and linework are three line weights. Every stair climbs the same way, towards the tail: the one lean. Sills and lower body (below the intake crease, rising to the tail as a wedge) are plain #100F0F and carry the sponsors. The nose is panels too, as hot as the band: fender noses, front fenders and bonnet, the bonnet vent, bumper corners and rear face in terracotta stairs, the intake strakes in terracotta. Number windows are plain black on each door (number_l, number_r) and the bonnet (number_top), clear of every panel, marked by stepped yellow corner brackets; the sim's light panel on each haunch gets the same window and brackets, a second display.
- **1 m:** the cut stair lines in the solid panels, the border rows and the cores, yellow inner lines in the nose and tail panels. The black base carries a tone-on-tone pixel weave in #1C1B1A. Race markings #DAD8CE on the black. 20-30 small roster marks in #DAD8CE grouped on the black, with a small wordmark heading a group where it fits: lower door, the duct behind the door (a wordmark over a row of marks), the rear bumper behind the rear wheel, and two black plates on the bonnet's front corners, never on a panel. Fireproof: none, or the flame mark once, mono #CF6A4C and small, on the rear face.

Swatches: #100F0F, #1C1B1A, #CF6A4C, #6B3528, #D0A215, #DAD8CE, #878580.
