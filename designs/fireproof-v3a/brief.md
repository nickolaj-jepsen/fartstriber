# fireproof-v3a

## The owner's brief

Fireproof v3. Nickolaj, 21 Oct 2026, in his words: "Can you help me do another take on the fireproof livery, i really like the pixel motif, but the rest i'm not really sold on, and i think a couple of factors fed into it not as good as i've hoped: 1. it was one of the first prototypes, before we've nailed down the workflow and preview tools 2. I was lazy with the feedback 3. Irrelevant \"requirements\" from the design skill, like having a huge focus on the wordmark (which would be relevant for a real sponsor, but for my own brand, i'd rather it just being cool-looking). So can you help me do another similar design, please avoid grounding it too much in the initial design, and use the color scheme from this file: github.com/nickolaj-jepsen/nixos modules/base/theme.nix"

What holds:

- A new Fireproof car on the kit mclaren-720s-gt3-evo, not a revision of fireproof-v2 or fireproof-stripes: keep the pixel motif (hard square pixels, stepped blocks, pixel dissolves, embers), drop their layout (orange car, yellow band nose to tail, yellow spine, black tail wedge, flame tongues).
- Palette: his Fireproof desktop theme (Flexoki-based). Neutrals: black #100F0F, bg #1C1B1A, bgAlt #282726, ui #343331, uiAlt #403E3C, muted #878580, fgAlt #B7B5AC, paper #DAD8CE, bright paper #F2F0E5. Accent #CF6A4C (terracotta) with its dark container #6B3528. The theme's identity is its warm dark greys, the paper and the terracotta accent.
- Cool-looking first. No wordmark-led layout and no big type: Fireproof is optional, at most one small discreet mark in one palette colour. The design leads. Small roster sponsors as the raced-car ecosystem, one colour per field.
- Number zones clear, on one solid field with contrast for the white number; edges clearly cross or avoid seams and shut lines.

## Concept

- **Source:** Favourite 24, Paul Miller Racing BMW M4 #1. Three colours meet as stacked rectangles of many sizes, each colour carries lighter and darker blocks of itself, the transition between colours is the whole design, and the number sits in one of the blocks. Carried into the theme: warm black #100F0F at the nose, terracotta #CF6A4C where the fields meet, paper #DAD8CE at the tail.
- **Expected (banned):** a warm-grey car with one band of even, single-size terracotta squares dissolving across the door between two clean plain fields: confetti on a plain car, one cell size, no layering, a bare nose. Nor v2 turned round in new colours: no dark tail with embers drifting forward, no straight split with pixels sprinkled along it.
- **The move:** there is no straight split and no plain field. The car changes from a black nose to a paper tail only through stacked rectangles in three sizes, with the terracotta core where they meet. The white number sits in a solid #6B3528 block of that collage.

References: 24, 26, 21

## Distances

- **50 m:** a dark front and a light back, with a hot terracotta collage where they meet. Black #100F0F and its tone own the bumper, nose fenders, bonnet and sills (about 45%). Paper #DAD8CE owns the haunch, engine cover, rear deck and wing (about 35%). Terracotta #CF6A4C with #6B3528 is the core (about 20%), across the rear of the front fender, the door, the pillars and the front of the roof, raked with the lean (top towards the tail) so the change reads as one big slash, not three vertical slices. From above: black bonnet, terracotta across the roof, paper engine cover.
- **10 m:** blocks axis-aligned in car space, in three tiers as seen on the side: about a quarter door, about 40 px, about 16 px. Layered like Paul Miller's: big blocks carry smaller blocks of their own tones (#282726 in the black, #6B3528 in the terracotta, #B7B5AC and #F2F0E5 in the paper); each colour reaches into its neighbours. Densest at the core, thinning to single strays on the bonnet front and rear deck, so the nose carries the system as fully as the tail. A black sill band and black lower bumpers ground the car; a terracotta pinline at the outline minimum runs along the top of the sill, stepping up and down where blocks meet it. Mirror shells, splitter lip and intake strakes terracotta. Wing endplates black with paper blocks. Number zones: a solid #6B3528 block on each door holds number_l and number_r with clearance; a solid #100F0F block sits under number_top on the bonnet and a solid #6B3528 block under the rear number on the paper rear face; no smaller block crosses any of them.
- **1 m:** the 16 px tier only in the core, as dust between the big blocks. Tone-on-tone horizontal scanline hairlines through the fields: #B7B5AC on the paper, #282726 on the black. Small Space Mono tech labels in #878580 on the paper haunch; tow arrows, the extinguisher E and the fuel mark in each field's contrast colour. 12-16 small roster sponsors, one colour per field: #DAD8CE on the black sills and nose corners (clear of the carbon side blade), #100F0F on the paper haunch behind the rear wheel and on the rear bumper. Fireproof: none, or the flame mark once, mono #CF6A4C and small, on the paper rear valance. At most eight swatches: #100F0F, #282726, #CF6A4C, #6B3528, #DAD8CE, #B7B5AC, #F2F0E5, #878580.
