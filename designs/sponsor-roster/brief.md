# Sponsor roster

Every roster brand on the McLaren in the groups a race car carries them: a row along each sill, a stack on each rear bumper corner, a row on the door under the headline, marks on the front fender and mirrors, and a few on the bonnet and engine cover. One colour per field: black marks on the white body, white marks on the black sill and lower band. Not a livery to race; it checks that each brand reads at its placed size beside the others and that no two look like one company.
