# zentiq-e: notes

Concept ranked 2 of 6 in the pairwise ranking; the owner took it forward (rated 2) with "The line across the top is a bit to straight compare to the more "natural" curves found elsewhere in the design". Refinement round 1 answered that: the coil over the roof is now an S. `livery check` passes; sim run 20261021-195100 shows the build. Baked views are not usable (the capture failed `livery bake check`, worst p95 60), so the flat views and the sim are the judges.

## The design

- **Base:** black `#0c0d0c`. Palette: acid `#b6ff00`, darker green `#3f6600`, belly grey `#cfd3c8`, charcoal `#202420`.
- **The body:** one ribbon of constant width as seen (92 px across: belly 20, scale field 48, black pin 14, acid lip 14), drawn per view from a spine in car space (`SIDE_L`, `_coil_top`, `SIDE_R`, plus `NECK_TOP`), Chaikin-smoothed.
  - The coil on top (the owner's note: it was a straight diagonal between two corners): square across the car at u 0.656 outboard of w -0.5 and at u 0.315 outboard of w 0.5, and between them one cubic Bezier S leaving and arriving square across, so the curvature changes all along and nothing runs straight, like the dips and rises on the sides. `coil_ease` 0.58 (end tangents as a share of the chord): it bends off the haunch, runs diagonally forward over the roof through about (0.52, 0.0) and bends again into the right A-pillar foot. Above 0.6 the middle runs along u beside two short shut lines on the roof's centre (u 0.52-0.55, w 0.03-0.07) and the lint snaps the lip and pin edges 8-9 px there (a visible notch), so 0.58 is the most bend that stays unsnapped. `coil_skew` 0 (symmetric; -0.3 makes a hook at the haunch, +0.3 a hook at the front). Its second bend (u 0.33-0.40, w 0.2-0.5) lies under the windscreen glass, so in the sim the body slips under the screen's right edge and comes out at the right A-pillar foot. Each piece runs past its view's edge and crosses that edge square (one u), so the views meet without a jog wherever the side's top edge lands in w.
  - Route: the neck leaves the head along the left bonnet flank (top, w about -0.53) and the left fender top, comes down behind the left front arch (u 0.30-0.35), dips under the left number (u 0.40-0.47, top edge at v 0.245), rises over the left haunch ahead of the light panel and crosses onto the top at u 0.656; on top it swings forward in an S over the engine cover and roof to u 0.315 at the right; it comes down behind the right front arch, dips under the right number, rises ahead of the right light panel and runs back over the right rear arch (spine v 0.94) to the tail.
  - The belly is the ribbon's front edge where it crosses from a side onto the top (`BELLY_SIDE`: left +1, top +1, right -1 as offsets left of travel in car space), so it is the same physical edge all the way round. That puts it under the left number on the left dip, at the sill on the right dip, and just above the right rear arch.
  - Scales: one row of acid kites down the field's middle (height 41 px, 1.8 long, pointing to the tail), small kites in the notches at both field edges; 7 px of darker green between them. Belly plates: black bars 7 px wide every 28 px. On side_r the scales are settled clear of the lint's lines (`geom.edges`), because the rise runs beside the door's rear shut line; on the other views they aren't, since settling pulls whole kites off the seams they cross.
  - Shed skin: four staggered rows of charcoal kites outside both edges, thinning out (density 0.75 at the edge), kept off the number zones and clear of the lint's lines (`geom.edges`).
  - ZENTIQ (roster wordmark, black, 0.024) on a plain acid stretch of the field: on the left dip under the number (u 0.385-0.505, set 0.004 towards the belly to clear the sill) and over the right haunch (u 0.72-0.84).
- **The head:** acid over the bonnet front to u 0.095 (u 0.06 outboard of |w| 0.5), cut out of the splitter (the mouth is painted black on its own), the bumper corners, grille uprights, the right intake bar and the fender nose pieces (the headlight surrounds). - **Hardware:** acid mirror shells with the Altmere mark in black.
- **Marks (13 partner placements):** grey on black sills behind the rising body (left Hexmoor, Huilec, Telvio marks; right Grivant, Ostrand, Drexa marks, 0.022 tall at v 0.09, u 0.665-0.77), Altmere on both mirrors, Rigmount wordmark and Norvane mark on the right door top inside the coil (v 0.72), Avelor and Brandvik marks either side of a ZENTIQ wordmark on the wing top facing rear. ## Constraints that shaped the layout

- **The roof's top maps disagree at its seams (seam waiver on `coil-scales.top` and `coil-plates.top`):** seam pairs between i102 (roof front panel) and i078 (roof) map to u 0.419-0.420 through i102 and u 0.424-0.427 through i078 (w 0.15-0.24), 0.005 u, about 8 px; between i078 and i104 (engine cover) to u 0.598-0.601 and 0.606-0.608 (w -0.29 to -0.34), 0.007 u. The scale pattern's 7 px gaps can't land on both sides, so the seam check failed (23 of 39 points). Within the kit's 0.01 seam tolerance; the baked top view shows no visible jog in the ribbon there. A kit re-fit of those two seams would let the waiver go.
- **Two short shut lines along the roof's centre** (top view, u 0.52-0.55 at w about 0.03 and 0.07, texture about 966-1014, 1168-1189): the coil's S can bend only so far (`coil_ease` up to 0.58) before its middle runs beside them and the lip and pin edges snap 8-9 px.
- **The windscreen glass** (unpainted, to about u 0.40 on the centre line and |w| 0.4 at u 0.33): the coil's second bend lies under it, because the right side's descent starts at u 0.315.
- **The door's rear shut line on side_r** (u 0.572 at v 0.3 to 0.599 at v 0.6) runs parallel to the body's rise: unsettled, a scale edge ran 49 px beside it (near-tangent, texture 1106, 596, i046).
- **The splitter top's top map** (i083) leaves an edge inside the island at u about -0.026: the head shape stopped there (near-tangent, texture 83, 1206), so the head is cut out of the splitter.

- **Number zones vs the sill:** under `number_l` / `number_r` (v from 0.288 / 0.295) the near-tangent reach leaves v 0-0.245 for the dip, a quarter of the side's height. The ribbon is a third (0.33 v), so at its lowest (u 0.42-0.47) its bottom 0.08 v runs below v 0: on the left the lip and part of the pinline, on the right the belly band, are cut there.
- **Over the right rear arch** the side is mapped only from the arch top (v 0.767 at u 0.86) to v 1.0, 0.23 v, and the top view maps the haunch only to |w| 0.55: the ribbon's upper part (pin and lip) is lost there.
- **The fender top over the front arch** is mapped by side_l only up to about v 0.85 and by the top view only to |w| 0.58: the neck shows as two partial pieces there (side lip sliver, top belly and field).
- **Door light panels** (u 0.712-0.772, v 0.45-0.70): both rises pass ahead of them with their rear edge at about u 0.69.
- **Side intake and carbon blade** (u 0.52-0.65, v 0.04-0.21): the rises start right behind the numbers so the ribbon passes above the intake hole; its lower edge still runs under the blade (occluder) at u 0.52-0.56.
- **Unmapped parts:** `rear_face` (i003) and `rear_valance` (i017) have no view map, so no sponsor can be anchored there: "zentiq on the rear face" sits on the wing top (i016) facing rear instead. `front_bumper_corner_l/r` (i021, i020) and both `wing_endplate` parts have no map either, so no marks on the bumper corners or endplates (and no front tow arrow: the bumper's side area is intake strakes i062/i159 and holes). The endplates and bumper corners are painted whole.
- **The headlights** are unpainted and sit under the bonnet's mapped edge; the bonnet map holds a whole eye only inboard of about |w| 0.27 at u 0.06, so the eyes sit there rather than on the headlights themselves.
- **No rotated sponsor or text:** `sponsor` and `type_block` follow the map's slope only, so ZENTIQ goes only where the body runs level (the left dip, the right haunch).
