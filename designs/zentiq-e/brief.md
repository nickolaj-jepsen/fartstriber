# Zentiq GT3: the mamba (zentiq-e)

## The owner's brief

Context: Nickolaj has not asked for this car in words. The plan's finish line needs a new livery made through the two checkpoints, so this brief is drawn from what he has said and rated so far; the concept pick is where he redirects it.

Nickolaj, in his words, on the cars so far:

- 18 Oct, on the first concepts: "they are just too boring". Then, on the Evangelion concepts: "a bit boring" (unit01), "I like the idea, but again a bit boring" (nerv); 19 Oct: "Better, but still a bit boring", "The side text is a bit boring", "The back design is nice, but the front is a bit boring".
- 19 Oct, on fireproof-v2's pixel burn: "I like the pixel design a lot, but it's totally missing from the front".
- 19 Oct, on eva-unit01: "it would look a lot better if there where some kind of \"stroke\" between the colors?"
- 19 Oct, with his favourite sim liveries (rows 21-34): "can you use this to improve your design-guidelines? Maybe take some learnings from them?"
- 19 Oct, on Fireproof: "just remove the wordmark (or keep it smaller/more descrete), it's not imporant for my own personal brand."
- 21 Oct, rating the finals: fireproof-v2 1, eva-unit01 2, eva-nerv 3.

A GT3 team car that could line up in GTWC 2026: Zentiq energy drink as the headline sponsor, in its acid green (#b6ff00) on black, with white or a light grey as the third field. Loud, dense and finished front to back, the way his favourites are: big shapes from 50 m, layers up close, and the transition between colours designed rather than a plain cut.

- **50 m:** one big idea that reads from the side, the front and the chase cam alike, with the front as strong as the back.
- **10 m:** where two fields meet, the edge is an event (a stroke, a dissolve or a raked cut), even in width along its whole length. Tones of each hue, pinlines, accent hardware (mirrors, splitter, wing endplates, intakes).
- **1 m:** fifteen or more small marks from the roster on sills, bumpers, mirrors and wing, sized to be read, the way a real team car carries its partners.

Constraints: the McLaren 720S GT3 Evo kit; the number zones stay clear and readable; Fireproof small or absent; text only in fonts from `assets/fonts`; sponsors only from `assets/sponsors` (roster brands as drawn and approved).

## The concept

- **Source:** a one-sentence story: Zentiq's mascot, an acid-green mamba, has wrapped itself once round the car and is looking out through the headlights.
- **Expected (banned):** the stock energy-drink car: black with green claw slashes, lightning bolts and a big wordmark on the door. Or a snake sticker on the door of a plain black car.
- **The move:** one animal, one body: a single unbroken ribbon of constant width, from the fangs at the splitter to the tail tip on the rear wing, coiling once round the black car. Nothing else on the car is big, and everything else serves the snake.

References: 23, 31

- **50 m:** the head is the nose: the front of the bonnet, the bumper and the headlight surrounds are green, with the eyes over the headlights. The top number stays on black beside the head. The body, about a third of the side's height, leaves the head over the left front fender and runs back low along the left door under the number. It rises over the left rear haunch, swings across the engine cover and roof in one S to the right side (curved all the way, like the sides, never a straight run: the owner's note on the sketch), comes down the right side and runs back along the right sill. It climbs the right rear bumper corner and ends with the tail tip on the right wing endplate. The ribbon stays clear of every number zone. The two sides differ, and the coil reads from the side, the front and the chase cam alike. zentiq in large black letters along the body on the left door and over the right haunch; the lockup on the bonnet beside the head; zentiq on the rear face.
- **10 m:** inside the ribbon, diamond scales in acid green and a darker green, biggest along its middle and smaller towards its edges, so the body has a spine and a direction. One edge of the ribbon is a light-grey belly band of even width crossed by black belly plates; the other edge is an even black pinline. The black car carries faint charcoal scales near the body, tone on tone, like shed skin. The car's own features are the snake's, as on Rawr: grey fangs down the splitter lip at the front corners, slit-pupil eyes and lids over the headlights, green mirror shells, scale-cut wing endplates. Black lower body on both sides.
- **1 m:** the smallest scales at the ribbon's edges, and hairlines on the belly plates. Marks in grey on the black field and in black on the belly band, grouped on both sills, the bumper corners, the mirrors, the wing endplates and the rear valance, fifteen or more. A small tech label on the B-pillar (the species name, a part number) in Space Mono; tow arrows and the extinguisher E. Fireproof absent.
