# Zentiq GT3, concept C: the bolt split

## The owner's brief

Context: Nickolaj has not asked for this car in words. The plan's finish line needs a new livery made through the two checkpoints, so this brief is drawn from what he has said and rated so far; the concept pick is where he redirects it.

Nickolaj, in his words, on the cars so far:

- 18 Oct, on the first concepts: "they are just too boring". Then, on the Evangelion concepts: "a bit boring" (unit01), "I like the idea, but again a bit boring" (nerv); 19 Oct: "Better, but still a bit boring", "The side text is a bit boring", "The back design is nice, but the front is a bit boring".
- 19 Oct, on fireproof-v2's pixel burn: "I like the pixel design a lot, but it's totally missing from the front".
- 19 Oct, on eva-unit01: "it would look a lot better if there where some kind of \"stroke\" between the colors?"
- 19 Oct, with his favourite sim liveries (rows 21-34): "can you use this to improve your design-guidelines? Maybe take some learnings from them?"
- 19 Oct, on Fireproof: "just remove the wordmark (or keep it smaller/more descrete), it's not imporant for my own personal brand."
- 21 Oct, rating the finals: fireproof-v2 1, eva-unit01 2, eva-nerv 3.

A GT3 team car that could line up in GTWC 2026: Zentiq energy drink as the headline sponsor, in its acid green (#b6ff00) on black, with white or a light grey as the third field. Loud, dense and finished front to back, the way his favourites are: big shapes from 50 m, layers up close, and the transition between colours designed rather than a plain cut.

- **50 m:** one big idea that reads from the side, the front and the chase cam alike, with the front as strong as the back.
- **10 m:** where two fields meet, the edge is an event (a stroke, a dissolve or a raked cut), even in width along its whole length. Tones of each hue, pinlines, accent hardware (mirrors, splitter, wing endplates, intakes).
- **1 m:** fifteen or more small marks from the roster on sills, bumpers, mirrors and wing, sized to be read, the way a real team car carries its partners.

Constraints: the McLaren 720S GT3 Evo kit; the number zones stay clear and readable; Fireproof small or absent; text only in fonts from `assets/fonts`; sponsors only from `assets/sponsors` (roster brands as drawn and approved).

## The concept

- **Source:** WRT #46 (row 25), its system transplanted: two fields that swap sides (black nose on the left, white nose on the right), a lime pinline tracing the split and the window line, hairlines across both fields, hazard chevrons, lime hardware and a big outlined designation across the roof. Here the fields are black and white and the pinline is acid green.
- **Expected (banned):** WRT with Zentiq in Monster's place: the same diagonal split behind the front wheel, the same vertical hairlines and a GEN-style wordmark on the door. Or any symmetric black car with a green beltline stripe.
- **The move:** the split is the Zentiq bolt. On each side the boundary between black and white is the brand's Z drawn the length of the car: back along the shoulder from the nose, down through the door in one steep jogged diagonal just behind the number, then back along the sill to the tail. An even acid-green pinline traces it its whole length, and the two sides swap colours across it.

References: 25, 22, 31

- **50 m:** On the left side the field ahead of and below the bolt (front bumper, front fender, the front of the door, and the sill strip behind the diagonal) is black; the field above and behind it (the shoulder strip over the front fender, the rear of the door, the haunch, the tail) is white. The right side has the same bolt with the colours swapped. The bolt's jog reads as the Zentiq mark at 50 m. On top the bonnet and roof split on the centreline, each half taking its side's nose colour, with a huge outlined Zentiq mark across the roof, as WRT draws its 46. zentiq on each haunch in the field's opposite colour, the lockup on the bonnet, zentiq on the rear face.
- **10 m:** One even-width acid-green pinline traces the whole bolt and the window line, then turns down the A-pillar to the splitter. WRT's hairlines run across both fields in the other field's tone (grey on white, dark grey on black), bending with the body like contour lines and densest near the split. Green hazard chevrons sit on the black front fender and bonnet corner, all leaning with the car. Hardware: green mirror shells, splitter lip, wing endplates and intake edges, with a black sill and lower bumper on both sides. Every number zone stays on black: the left side's number sits on black at the front of the door; wherever a number lands on white (the right door, the right half of the bonnet) it gets a black plate keyed in green.
- **1 m:** WRT's tech text: a raked code line (ZQ-26 and the like) in Space Mono beside each number, the extinguisher E and tow arrows. In each field the marks are one colour, white on black and black on white: rows along the sills, the nose corners and the rear bumper, both mirrors and both endplates, and a stack behind each front arch, fifteen or more. Fireproof absent.
