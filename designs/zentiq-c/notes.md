# zentiq-c: notes

Build, 21 Oct: the concept that ranked first of six, built for the owner's concept pick with only what `livery check` and the sim shots needed. `livery check` passes; sim run `20261021-190807` shows this build. No critic round yet.

## The design

Colours: black `#111214`, white `#efefeb`, acid green `#b6ff00`; hairline tones grey `#bdbfbc` (on white) and dark grey `#3a3d42` (on black). Base white.

- **50 m, the bolt split.** On each side the black/white boundary is the Zentiq Z (`_bolt`): a top bar along the shoulder at `shoulder_v` 0.72 from the nose, a diagonal leaning `lean` 20° (top to the tail) just behind the number, jogged `jog` 0.04 u towards the tail at `jog_v` 0.44 (upper piece through `diag_u` 0.515 there), and a bottom bar at `sill_v` 0.26 back to the tail. Left: black ahead of and below it. Right: the same polygon, colours swapped. `jog` was chosen on a wedge (0.02 / 0.04 / 0.06): 0.02 reads as a kink, 0.06 starts to look like two splits. `shoulder_v` went from 0.70 to 0.72 so the pin's lower edge clears the fender nose's top edge (below).
- **Top.** Split on the centreline: ahead of the roof / cover seam (`SEAM_U` 0.605) left black, right white (each side's nose colour); behind it swapped (each side's tail colour), so front and chase cam both read half and half. The right side's skin seen from above is black (its field above the bolt), the left's white. The roof carries the Zentiq mark `roof_h` 0.18 long at `roof_u` 0.495, reading from the front, outlined in green and counter-filled (white on the black half, black on the white half), as WRT draws its 46.
- **10 m.**
  - One 18 px green pin (`pin`) everywhere: centred on the bolt, under the window line nose to tail (`_window_pin`, measured at the door's texture scale at each point of the line, so it is 18 px all along), down the top's centreline, ahead of the roof / cover seam, inside the bonnet's outline (the A-pillar-to-splitter turn) and the rear face's outline, and round the bonnet and cover plates.
  - Hairlines (`hair` 9 px) parallel to the diagonal, the first `hair_g0` 0.012 from it, each gap `hair_r` 1.15 times the last, `hair_n` 16 each way, bowing `hair_bow` 0.02 at sill and window, `hair_gap` 14 px clear of the pin and clear of the number; on top, lines along the car opening out from the centreline split. Each in the other field's tone, swapping where they cross a split. The side hairlines are kept clear of the lint's lines with a stricter `geom.edges` (margin 3 px, run margin 10 px), because the ones near the door's rear shut line lean almost with it.
  - Green chevrons (`patterns.chevrons`): on the left, three (`chev_step` 0.022, `chev_half` 0.08, apex at `chev_mid` v 0.33 from `chev_u0` 0.31) on the black panel behind the front wheel, all ahead of the door's front edge; on the right, in the black shoulder strip.
  - Hardware: green splitter, mirror shells, wing endplates and intake edges; black sills, intake strakes, wing, rear face and valance; each bumper corner in its side's nose colour over a `chin_px` 90 px black-and-green hazard chin along its foot with a green pin on top, so both noses have a dark lower edge. 90 was chosen on a wedge against 40: the taller chin carries the hazard up the front bumper in front34 and side_r.
  - Numbers: the left door's number sits on black; the right door's gets a black plate keyed 14 px green; the rear face's on black is keyed the same. The two `number_panels` islands (never seen) are black so the sim's numbers on them have contrast.
- **1 m.** On the rear door and haunch one type system in the field's opposite colour: the zentiq wordmark (`head_h` 0.038 at `head_u` 0.635, `head_v` 0.66), and under it on its start edge ZQ-26 and 720S GT3 EVO in Space Mono, sheared 12°. The lockup on a green-keyed black plate across the bonnet's nose; zentiq on a black plate across the engine cover reading from behind. Small marks, one colour per field: Huilec on the panel behind the front wheel and Grivant on the lower door (`sill_row_v` 0.15), split at the door's front edge (Huilec ends `sill_gap` 0.01 ahead of it, Grivant starts `sill_back` 0.015 behind it); Telvio and Ostrand behind the side intake, Rigmount and Avelor on the rear bumper corner, Brandvik and Altmere on the grille uprights, Bellcrest on each mirror base, Drexa and Hexmoor behind the right front arch; tow arrows on the rear corners, the extinguisher E under each mirror. 20 small marks. Fireproof absent.

## Constraints that shaped the layout

- **Shoulder bar:** between the front wheel arch top (v 0.767 at u 0.205, `front_arch_l`) and the fender nose i120's top edge (v 0.70-0.71 at u 0.08-0.11), a pin centred at 0.70 left its lower edge 11 px from i120's edge (near-tangent, side_l). At 0.72 it clears. Above, the right door's number plate (number box v 0.295-0.534 plus 24 px of plate, to v about 0.62) keeps the bar from going lower than about 0.66.
- **Window pin:** `window_line_l` sits 0.011-0.0135 v (4-5 px) above i128's mapped top edge at u 0.37-0.40 (0.826 against 0.8125-0.815; the right side agrees within 0.003-0.008), so a pin 18 px under the feature line, at one scale for the whole line, ran 11-12 px from the paint edge there. Measured at the local scale it passes; the pin still shows a few px narrower there than on the right.
- **Left chevrons:** i128's map also claims the panel behind the front wheel (i158, u 0.29-0.40) and its texture there is never seen, so chevrons that crossed the door's front edge stopped at 6 of 12 seam points. They now sit wholly on i158, below v 0.41, where the door's front edge leans back (u 0.346 at v 0.5 to 0.396 at v 0.317).
- **Sill row:** the door's front edge (u 0.405 at v 0.15) cut Grivant's G (left) and T (right). The panel ahead of it stands proud of the door, and from front34 it hides the first 0.01 u behind the edge. Grivant fits behind it only up to `sill_back` about 0.015: at 0.02-0.025 the field (to u 0.515, the carbon blade beyond) is shorter than the wordmark at its 20 px minimum.
- **Right bumper chin:** a shut line crosses i020 (front_bumper_corner_r) about 70 px above its foot; the chin's pin at 60 px ran along it (near-tangent). At 90 px (and 40) it clears.
- **Roof mark:** `sponsor` places a variant filled in one colour; it can't outline it or fill its counter per field. The mark's 14 outline points are copied from `assets/sponsors/zentiq/mark.svg` into `MARK` and drawn as geometry. A library primitive for a sponsor variant as a car-space shape (to outline or split) would replace the copy.
- **Nose, side views:** side_l / side_r map nothing at u 0.035-0.1, v 0.3-0.75 (chevrons there drew nothing) and nothing at (0.06, 0.2) (a tow arrow there was refused), so the front fender's chevrons sit behind the arch and the tow arrows on the rear corners.
- **Bonnet:** between the series zone (u 0.01-0.09, where the lockup plate is) and the vent (u 0.10-0.18, |w| < 0.24) the bonnet is too short for a chevron lane; chevrons at u 0.2 drew nothing (the windscreen starts there). So the bonnet has no chevrons.
- **Rear face:** mapped by no view, so `sponsor` can't place zentiq on it; the chase cam's zentiq is on the engine cover instead.
- **Side intake:** its opening (u 0.573-0.65, v 0.04-0.21) and the carbon blade (u 0.52-0.61, v 0.08-0.21) put the bolt's bottom bar at v 0.26, above them, rather than on the sill.
- **Code line:** beside the number there is only about 0.05 u (number back edge u 0.471 to the diagonal), and above it the shoulder; so the code lines sit under the haunch wordmark.
- **Sill rows:** `sponsor_group` refused four wordmarks in u 0.315-0.515 at v 0.15 (minimum heights 0.013-0.019); the row is two wordmarks there and two marks behind the intake (u 0.655-0.77).
- **Series door box:** the haunch type block (zentiq and the code lines) sits in the template's series sponsor box (`series_door_l/_r`, u 0.484-0.754, v 0.25-0.78; lint warning only). The sim drew nothing over it in the shots.
- **Corbaux** is large-only, so it has no place in this layout's rows.
