## The design

Colours: black `#111214`, white `#efefeb`, acid green `#b6ff00`; hairline tones grey `#bdbfbc` (on white) and dark grey `#3a3d42` (on black). Base white.

- **50 m, the bolt split.** On each side the black/white boundary is the Zentiq Z (`_bolt`): a top bar along the door's shoulder at `shoulder_v` 0.75 from the front fender's rear edge (`fender_rear_edge_l/_r`, half a pin behind it, so the centred pin there runs from the shut line back onto the door), a diagonal leaning `lean` 20° (top to the tail) just behind the number, jogged `jog` 0.04 u towards the tail at `jog_v` 0.44 (upper piece through `diag_u` 0.515 there), and a bottom bar at `sill_v` 0.26 back to the tail. The whole front fender (`front_panel_l/_r`: skin, nose piece and slats) is in the field ahead of the Z, painted as a part so the side and top views give it one colour: black on the left, white on the right. Left: black ahead of and below the Z. Right: the same polygon, colours swapped. `jog` was chosen on a wedge (0.02 / 0.04 / 0.06): 0.02 reads as a kink, 0.06 starts to look like two splits. The bar used to run from the nose over the fender crest, where it tore in every sim run (below); starting it at the fender's rear edge also gives the Z top and bottom bars of similar length, as the mark has.
- **Top.** Split on the centreline: ahead of the roof / cover seam (`SEAM_U` 0.605) left black, right white (each side's nose colour); behind it swapped (each side's tail colour), so front and chase cam both read half and half. ## Constraints that shaped the layout

- **Fender crest, side / top ownership:** each fender skin (i123 left, i072 right) is mapped by the side only up to side v 0.708-0.79 (u 0.094-0.142) and above the arch only in a thin band over its top (v 0.767-0.80), the top owning the rest, in a boundary stepped on the kit's 4 px grid; the fender nose (i120, i086) ends at side v 0.70-0.71, and the five slats per side (i137/i130/i125/i122/i121, i085/i095/i100/i105/i108) lie along that crest. A field edge or pin there tore in every sim run (`20261021-200211` to `-212041`): centred at v 0.72 it left notches, at 0.75 with the side map carried 40 px over the top-owned texels its lower edge broke into teeth and fragments from the A-pillar foot to the headlight. So no edge crosses the crest: the front fenders are painted whole (`front_panel_l/_r`) and the Z's top bar starts at the fender's rear edge. A kit whose side map kept the fender skin up to the crest would let the bar run from the nose, as the brief first drew it.
- **Fender side face:** the side view maps the fender skin (i123) only in a band over the arch (u 0.10-0.28, about 0.1-0.15 v tall) and the nose piece (i120) round the headlight. Raked hairlines there are cut into pieces of 150-420 px; `geom.edges().settle` drops all but one or two, and the ones it keeps on i120 showed as blobs in the texture, so the fender's side face carries none: from front34 the black face between the top's hairlines and the headlight stays plain.
- **Window pin, screen width:** a strip of constant car-space height under the window line and over the haunch showed on screen at 9-10 px over the haunch and at the B-pillar's foot (u 0.555-0.6) against 10-12 px on the door (side_l and side_r, runs `20261021-221929`, `-224139`): the side map's v there is not the side camera's height. `haunch_k` makes up for it by eye. The side map's top also sits 0.007-0.01 v under `window_line_l/_r` along the whole door, so the pin's top quarter there falls on glass.
- **Haunch tops:** the side map of i128 / i046 reaches side v 0.915-1.0 at u 0.62-0.86 and falls to 0.825 by u 0.92; the window line rises to v 1.0 at u 0.66, above it. A pin on the window line there sat on the side / top boundary and showed as green fragments from above; it now follows the side map's edge (above).
- **Window line:** `window_line_l` sits 0.011-0.0135 v above i128's side-mapped top at u 0.37-0.40.
- **Shoulder bar:** the right door's number plate (keyed to side v 0.614) keeps the bar from going lower than about 0.66; the bar stays at 0.75, on the door only (above). The thin white (left) / black (right) shoulder strip on the door is what lies between its pin and the window pin.
