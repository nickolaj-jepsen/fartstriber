The owner accepted the concept (rated 3, no note). As it stands, `livery check` passes (0 errors, 27 warnings) and sim run 20261021-201950 shows the build. Baked views are unusable (the kit's capture failed `livery bake check` at 18:52 on 21 Oct, p95 60 on side_l materials), so values were judged on the flat views and the sim shots.

Every word and mark reads whole in 20261021-201950. Still open, from the critic's round-1 `lint_missed`: the sim's light panel sits about a letter's width from the door zentiq's q (side_l) and z (side_r). ## The design

- **Palette (6 swatches):** light grey `#d5d7d3` with tone `#a3a6a2`; Zentiq acid green `#b6ff00` with tone `#78b000`; black `#111111` with charcoal `#393a39`. Base black.
- **Mosaic (`Mosaic`, one `geom.fill` per view, `_mosaic_fill`):** cut out of every part painted on its own (`WHOLE`: the lower parts, splitter top and lip, mirrors, bonnet vent and the four unmapped parts), so its edge ends on their outlines. Every block is an axis-aligned rectangle in car space, its edges snapped to a 0.004 seen grid, painted in order so later blocks overlap earlier ones (what shows of each colour is computed back to front).
  - Ground: rows 0.03 seen high of 0.012 u cells from u -0.06 to 1.26, each in its zone's main colour, so each zone boundary steps row by row and is never a clean line. It runs past the wing's trailing edge so it never ends inside the wing top.
  - Tiers (seen height, candidates, density at a transition, density in a zone's body): big 0.075 / 70 / 0.85 / 0.25, med 0.034 / 260 / 0.9 / 0.18, small 0.015 / 700 / 0.75 / 0.06 (top: twice the candidates). Lengths 2:1 to 6:1, heights ±25%. The black zone's body carries `black_tone` 1.8× the blocks, the grey zone's `grey_tone` 1.8× and the green zone's `green_tone` 1.8×, so no zone (tail, nose or door) is a plain field (density only gates blocks; every block's random draws happen either way, so the seed's layout holds).
  - Inside a transition (within `reach` 0.1 u of its centre, or `low_reach` 0.09 v of the lower band's top) a block takes either zone's colour along a linear ramp, 60% main, 40% tone, densest at the centre. In a zone's body, blocks are the zone's tone (`tone` 0.6) or main colour; `stray` 0.25 of the small ones take a foreign colour (the dead pixels).
  - Sides: grey to green at `t1` 0.33, green to black at `t2` 0.68 (wedged 0.66-0.74: 0.68 keeps the headline on green and the tail black), lower black band to `low_v` 0.27. Top: bonnet to roof at `t1_top` 0.24, roof to engine cover at `t2_top` 0.64 for |w| < `flank_w` 0.5; outside it the fender and haunch tops follow the sides' t1/t2.
  - Seed 3, picked from a 1-6 seed sheet: clearest grey nose with black blocks, and a black tail.
- **Blocks of their own (top layer of the mosaic):** each side number in a black block u 0.392-0.488 (`NUM_U`), v 0.235-0.575, with a charcoal step under it and a grey partner plate on top of it with the same edges (Paul Miller's WeatherTech plate over the 1), as tall as Telvio's wordmark plus `np_pad` 0.0055 on every side; a green plate behind the headline, reaching `sub_drop` 0.08 v further down to hold its small line, laid with `plate_n` 40 big and medium blocks of its own, `plate_tone` 0.55 of them the darker green (black type reads on both tones; round 1's critic called the flat plate the plainest field on the car); a black block behind the front wheel (u 0.29-0.372, v 0.22-0.5, from inside the arch across the door's front edge) for a stack of two marks; black blocks under the sill rows (u 0.40-0.525 and 0.632-0.776, v 0.2-0.31); a charcoal block over the rear arch for the tech label; a three-plate stack behind the rear wheel (u 0.952-1.01: green, grey, charcoal); on top, a black block round the top number (u 0.095-0.182, w 0.235-0.45) and its twin across the vent (w -0.49 to -0.235) for a partner mark, a grey plate under the bonnet lockup and a green block under the roof wordmark (u 0.41-0.49, the wordmark's width plus 0.03 w each side).
- **Parts no view maps:** rear face black and both wing endplates grey, cut with texture-rectangle blocks in three sizes (`_texture_blocks`, each block pulled clear of the lint's lines with `geom.edges(kit, *geom.MARGINS).settle`, or dropped); the front bumper corners grey with grey-tone, black and green blocks, so the nose reads grey from the front. Splitter lip, mirror shells and bonnet vent green (the vent in the accent so the two black bonnet blocks read apart from it; black, it merged with the 451 block into one bar in round 1's front34); splitter top, sills, sill strips, crash opening, grille uprights and sides, intake edges, valance, deck lip, swan necks and number panels black.
- **Type and sponsors:** zentiq wordmark black on each door, `head_h` 0.058 at (0.6, 0.64), with ENERGY RACING under it in Orbitron Bold (zentiq's own face), black, em `sub_em` 0.017, its top `sub_gap` 0.03 v under the wordmark's ink box, starting on the wordmark's start edge (the tail end on side_r) and tracked to end on its other edge: one hierarchy on the door; the zentiq lockup black on the bonnet at u 0.05; zentiq black on the roof and green on the wing top, both facing rear. ## Constraints that shaped the layout

- **Rear face (`rear_face`, i003) is mapped by no view**, so no sponsor or type can go on it: the chase-cam zentiq sits on the wing top instead (top view u 1.04), inside the wing's series keep-clear block (a warning, not strict). The rear face's blocks are texture rectangles.
- **Bumper corners (`front_bumper_corner_l/_r`) and wing endplates** are unmapped too: their blocks are rectangles in texture space, so they are horizontal on the car only where the island isn't rotated in the texture. In sim 20261021-185903 (front34, rear34) the bumper corners read grey with level blocks and the endplates grey with green blocks.
- **Tech label room on the haunch:** in side_l, type over the rear arch shows whole only from about u 0.79 to 0.86 between v 0.78 and 0.89; "ZQ-720/GT3" at u 0.885, v 0.83-0.9 was refused (91% shown), and a second line at v 0.776, u 0.834 too (68%). So the label is one short line. Labels under 0.0165 u fail `min_text_px` there.
- **Engine cover centre at u 0.84-0.89, |w| < 0.14** is unseen from the top; the deck sponsors sit at u 0.72-0.80.
- **Ostrand's mark** needs 0.0222 u at the rear bumper side; the stack marks are 0.025.
- **Wing top too short for wordmarks beside the zentiq:** a sponsor is clipped to the island it anchors on, and the wing top's map ends at about |w| 0.68. Ostrand's wordmark needs 0.0216 car lengths of height there (about 0.52 w long); at w ±0.52 it was cut to "TRAND" and Rigmount to "RIGM" (sim 20261021-201634, rear34 and top). So the wing carries marks.
- **Bonnet partner beside the vent:** a wordmark along w doesn't fit between the vent's left edge (about w -0.27 in the top view, where the right edge is about 0.22) and the bonnet's edge. Huilec's wordmark at its 0.0142 minimum came out "HUIL", clipped at the vent. Hence the mark.
- **Under the door, v < 0.2 faces down:** square-on, side_l's lower body edge is just under the sill rows (v 0.2-0.28), so no mark row can go lower; the rows sit on black blocks at the door's foot.
- **Partner sizes:** Telvio's wordmark needs 0.0169 car lengths at the side number (so the plate's padding is at most 0.006 of the block's 0.096 u width); a Grivant + Avelor mark column needs 0.043 car lengths (0.23 v) behind the front wheel.
- **No room for a tow arrow on the sides:** at the nose (side u 0.07, v 0.3) it painted nowhere any shot sees (the bumper corner is unmapped, `front_bumper_corner_l/_r`), with no lint warning; under the plate stack (u 0.975, v 0.25) the body's lower edge cut it to a blob in side_l (sim 20261021-194123). The rear face that would carry it is unmapped (`rear_face`, i003). Dropped.
- **Front sill row, boxed in three ways:** the side number zone (`number_l` u 0.408-0.471 from v 0.288; on side_l Huilec at v 0.245 or higher still overlapped it by 112-216 px, a strict error) above; the sill's lower edge below (at v 0.235 with the 0.115 u row, the letters' feet were cut by the body edge in side_l); and in front34 a body fold behind the front arch, which hid Huilec's H with the row starting at u 0.405 or 0.42 (it read "IUILEC"; whole from u 0.435, sim 20261021-185903). Shorter than 0.09 u, Huilec drops under its minimum height (0.013 car lengths).
- **Maps that stop short of their outlines:** `sill_strip_l/_r` (i174, i045: the mosaic ended about 11 px inside, at v -0.004, u 0.51-0.53), `grille_upright_l` (i147, u -0.014) and `splitter_top` (i083, u -0.026). These parts are painted whole and the mosaic is cut out of them.
- **`geom.snapped` raised on the top mosaic** (an `AttributeError: 'NoneType' object has no attribute 'intersection'` in `Edges.alongside`, called from `_stretch` with a `_band` that came back None, likely `ring_part` on a closed line), so the mosaic is not snapped; it is cut out of the whole-painted parts instead. A library fault to propose, not a design constraint now.
