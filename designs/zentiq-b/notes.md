## The design

- **Palette (6 swatches):** light grey `#d5d7d3` with tone `#a3a6a2`; Zentiq acid green `#b6ff00` with tone `#78b000`; black `#111111` with charcoal `#393a39`. Base black.
- **Mosaic (`Mosaic`, one `geom.fill` per view, `_mosaic_fill`):** cut out of every part painted on its own (`WHOLE`: the lower parts, splitter top and lip, mirrors, bonnet vent and the five unmapped parts), so its edge ends on their outlines. Every block is an axis-aligned rectangle in car space, its edges snapped to a 0.004 seen grid, painted in order so later blocks overlap earlier ones (what shows of each colour is computed back to front).
  - Ground: rows 0.03 seen high of 0.012 u cells from u -0.06 to 1.26, each in its zone's main colour, so each zone boundary steps row by row and is never a clean line. It runs past the wing's trailing edge so it never ends inside the wing top.
  - Tiers (seen height, candidates, density at a transition, density in a zone's body): big 0.075 / 70 / 0.85 / 0.25, med 0.034 / 260 / 0.9 / 0.18, small 0.015 / 700 / 0.75 / 0.06 (top: twice the candidates). Lengths 2:1 to 6:1, heights ±25%. The black zone's body carries `black_tone` 1.8× the blocks, the grey zone's `grey_tone` 1.8× and the green zone's `green_tone` 1.8×, so no zone (tail, nose or door) is a plain field (density only gates blocks; every block's random draws happen either way, so the seed's layout holds).
  - Inside a transition (within `reach` 0.1 u of its centre, or `low_reach` 0.09 v of the lower band's top) a block takes either zone's colour along a linear ramp, 60% main, 40% tone, densest at the centre. In a zone's body, blocks are the zone's tone (`tone` 0.6) or main colour; `stray` 0.25 of the small ones take a foreign colour (the dead pixels).
  - Sides: grey to green at `t1` 0.33, green to black at `t2` 0.68 (wedged 0.66-0.74: 0.68 keeps the headline on green and the tail black), lower black band to `low_v` 0.27. Top: bonnet to roof at `t1_top` 0.24, roof to engine cover at `t2_top` 0.64 for |w| < `flank_w` 0.38 (the engine cover, |w| < 0.365); outside it the fender and haunch tops follow the sides' t1 and break into black at `flank_t2` 0.76 (wedged 0.68-0.84), later than the sides, so the haunch tops beside the cover stay green in blocks well past the door window (round 2's critic saw them meet the green side in one clean raked line on the shoulder crease).

## Constraints that shaped the layout

- **Rear face (`rear_face`, i003) and rear valance (`rear_valance`, i017) are mapped by no view**, so no sponsor, mark or type can go on them: the chase-cam zentiq sits on the wing top instead (top view u 1.04), inside the wing's series keep-clear block (a warning, not strict). Their blocks are texture rectangles. This is what keeps round 2's ask for a partner row across the rear bumper from being drawn.
- **Rear deck (`rear_deck`, i092) shows only as a sliver** at the top view's tail edge (about u 0.96-1.0) under the wing: a row of the Altmere mark and Huilec wordmark at u 0.965, |w| < 0.22, facing rear, came out as clipped fragments in the flat top view and was dropped.
- **Bumper corners (`front_bumper_corner_l`, i021, and `_r`, i020)** are the big grey faces under the headlights in front34 and side_l (the body model's evidence: 17557 px of i021 in side_l, u 0-0.133, v 0.08-0.63), but no side view maps them; i021 has only a top map (u 0.045-0.17, w -0.66..-0.53) and i020 none. So no partner mark can go on the nose corners. Their blocks are texture rectangles, horizontal on the car only where the island isn't rotated in the texture.
- **Nose top too broken up for marks:** partner marks on the top view either side of the bonnet lockup were cut by the bonnet's edge seam at (u 0.05, |w| 0.28, height 0.016-0.022) and (u 0.072, |w| 0.28); at (0.075, |w| 0.27, 0.018) they were whole in the flat view but read in the top and front34 shots (sim 20261021-210643) as a cut half-circle and three ticks; at (0.22, |w| 0.56) on the fender tops they were clipped to fragments; (0.25, 0.42) lands on no mapped island. Dropped.
- **Tech label room on the haunch:** in side_l, type over the rear arch shows whole only from about u 0.79 to 0.86 between v 0.78 and 0.89; "ZQ-720/GT3" at u 0.885, v 0.83-0.9 was refused (91% shown), and a second line at v 0.776, u 0.834 too (68%). So the label is one short line. Labels under 0.0165 u fail `min_text_px` there.
- **Engine cover centre at u 0.84-0.89, |w| < 0.14** is unseen from the top; the deck sponsors sit at u 0.72-0.80.
- **Ostrand's mark** needs 0.0222 u at the rear bumper side; the stack marks are 0.025.
- **Wing top too short for wordmarks beside the zentiq:** a sponsor is clipped to the island it anchors on, and the wing top's map ends at about |w| 0.68. Ostrand's wordmark at w ±0.52 was cut to "TRAND" and Rigmount to "RIGM" (sim 20261021-201634). So the wing carries marks.
- **Bonnet partner beside the vent:** a wordmark along w doesn't fit between the vent's left edge (about w -0.27 in the top view) and the bonnet's edge; Huilec's wordmark at its 0.0142 minimum came out "HUIL". Hence the mark.
- **Under the door, v < 0.2 faces down:** square-on, side_l's lower body edge is about v 0.2 under the door; marks reaching v 0.198 had their feet cut there (sim 20261021-210013), so DREXA sits at v 0.247 on a black block at the door's foot.
- **Front sill row, boxed in four ways:** the side number zone (`number_l` u 0.408-0.471 from v 0.288) above; the body's lower edge (about v 0.2) below; in front34 a body fold behind the front arch, which hid a row's first letter with the row starting at u 0.405 or 0.42 (whole from u 0.435, sim 20261021-185903); and in rear34 a crease that bent the first mark 28% with the row starting at u 0.435 on side_r (lint `crease`). With the row ending on the number block's tail edge (u 0.488), that leaves u 0.455-0.488 by v 0.2-0.288: no wordmark fits at its minimum height (Huilec's needs 0.0625 u), and two marks at 0.0152 (Huilec's mark needs 0.015, the most the height allows is about 0.0167) read as two dots from the side camera (round 3, sim 20261021-211818). - **No room for a tow arrow on the sides:** at the nose (side u 0.07, v 0.3) it painted nowhere any shot sees (the bumper corner is unmapped), with no lint warning; under the plate stack (u 0.975, v 0.25) the body's lower edge cut it to a blob in side_l (sim 20261021-194123). Dropped.

- **`geom.snapped` raised on the top mosaic** (an `AttributeError: 'NoneType' object has no attribute 'intersection'` in `Edges.alongside`, called from `_stretch` with a `_band` that came back None, likely `ring_part` on a closed line), so the mosaic is not snapped; it is cut out of the whole-painted parts instead. A library fault to propose.
