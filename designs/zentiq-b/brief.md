## The owner's brief

A GT3 team car that could line up in GTWC 2026: Zentiq energy drink as the headline sponsor, in its acid green (#b6ff00) on black, with white or a light grey as the third field. Loud, dense and finished front to back, the way his favourites are: big shapes from 50 m, layers up close, and the transition between colours designed rather than a plain cut.

- **50 m:** one big idea that reads from the side, the front and the chase cam alike, with the front as strong as the back.
- **10 m:** where two fields meet, the edge is an event (a stroke, a dissolve or a raked cut), even in width along its whole length. Tones of each hue, pinlines, accent hardware (mirrors, splitter, wing endplates, intakes).
- **1 m:** fifteen or more small marks from the roster on sills, bumpers, mirrors and wing, sized to be read, the way a real team car carries its partners.

Constraints: the McLaren 720S GT3 Evo kit; the number zones stay clear and readable; Fireproof small or absent; text only in fonts from `assets/fonts`; sponsors only from `assets/sponsors` (roster brands as drawn and approved).

## This concept

- **Source:** Paul Miller Racing #1 (row 24), its system transplanted: three colours as blocks that dissolve into each other, so the transition between them is the design. The blocks are rectangles of many sizes, stretched along the car and overlapping in layers, and the number sits in a block of its own. Here light grey at the nose, acid green through the door and black from the haunch back.
- **Expected (banned):** fireproof-v2 recoloured: square pixels of one grid size dissolving along one raked line between two plain fields. Or a black car with green squares scattered evenly like wallpaper.
- **The move:** nothing but horizontal rectangles. Every edge on the car, between fields and inside them, is horizontal or vertical in car space. Nowhere outside the type is there a diagonal, a curve or a clean line between two colours, and no field is plain: each zone is itself a mosaic of its own tones.

References: 24, 26

- **50 m:** three zones from nose to tail, each running into the next over about a fender's length: light grey over the nose (bumper, bonnet, front fenders), acid green through the door, and black from the rear haunch back over the engine cover, rear deck and tail and along the whole lower body. On top, the grey bonnet breaks into green at the windscreen, and the green roof breaks into black over the engine cover. zentiq in large black letters across the green door behind the number; the lockup in black on the grey bonnet for the front; zentiq in green on the black tail for the chase cam. Black stays the largest field.
- **10 m:** blocks in three sizes, the biggest half a door tall and the smallest a hand wide, 2:1 to 6:1 long, laid in overlapping layers as on the Paul Miller car. At each transition the two colours' blocks interleave, densest in the middle of the zone and thinning both ways. Each zone also carries blocks in tones of its own colour: two greys in the nose, a darker green in the green, charcoal in the black. A dark lower-body band ties the sills and lower bumpers together. Accent hardware: green mirror shells, a green splitter lip, grey wing endplates cut with green blocks. Each side number sits in a black block of its own at the front of the door, and the top number in a black block on the grey bonnet.
- **1 m:** the smallest blocks appear as single stray cells far from the transitions, like dead pixels. Tech labels in Space Mono (part numbers, a telemetry readout) sit on blocks. The roster runs in rows on the black sills, the nose corners, the mirrors, the wing endplates and the rear bumper, with a block-shaped stack of plates behind the rear wheel: fifteen or more marks, one colour per block. Fireproof absent.
