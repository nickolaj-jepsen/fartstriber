## The brief

NERV HQ during an Angel attack: a hexagon emergency display bursts out of the nose and burns down to the tail along the car's rake, framed low by hazard tape. Large condensed type like the show's title cards and tactical displays; text naming the show's ideas is fine.

- **50 m:** one big idea, the hexagon display. Over the nose the car is mostly orange; at the tail it is black, framed low by the hazard tape.
- **10 m:** one system. The cells come in three sizes and three tones (orange, burnt orange, charcoal), so no plain black area larger than a door is left. A red pinline traces the tape and the chevron lane, and its ends break into raked dashes. The type sits in fields the system makes. Red marks the car's features: the splitter lip, the intakes, the mirrors, the wing endplates and the fender slats. Everything leans the same way, with three line weights.
- **1 m:** small tactical marks in a group, the charcoal cells on the rear face, the plate's orange keyline.
- **Type, one system:**
  - **Plates:** a plate is sized to its text, with equal padding on every side, and leans 45°.
  - **Stacks:** the lines in a stack share a left edge and one line gap.
  - **Type:** one font and tracking for the labels; EMERGENCY's letter-per-cell spacing is the one deliberate exception.
  - **Clipping:** nothing is clipped by an island outline or an arch.

Constraints: near-black, orange, signal red and white, with burnt orange and charcoal as the orange's cooling tones; the number zones stay clear and readable; the hazard diagonals clearly cross seams and shut lines rather than running alongside them; text only in fonts from `assets/fonts`. No traced or redrawn Evangelion or NERV logos.
