# eva-unit01: notes

The design Nickolaj accepted on 19 Oct, ported onto the v2 library and the refitted kit (version 2) on 20 Oct, then refined in the 20 Oct autonomous rounds. Each round's verdict is in `verdicts.jsonl`; what changed and why is in git.

## The design

Unit-01's armour on violet, after his reference (`references.md` row 32). Colours: violet `#4b2a7b`, dark violet `#1d0a3a`, acid green `#8ee53f`, dark green `#5c9e2a`, orange `#f46200`, near-black `#16121c`. No wordmark or logo. Parts are named from the kit's body model; the one island id left is the roof's front panel `i102` (the part `roof` also takes i078's pillars and window frames, which stay violet).

- **50 m:** green plates on violet: the front fenders (each one whole green plate, `front_panel_*` painted green as islands, from the nose to a raked end through `blade_u1` 0.22 at `blade_v0` 0.58, short of the door shut line; the raked cut at the nose, `blade_u0` -0.2, sits ahead of the car), the bonnet's centre pentagon, the door's lightning-cut plate (`door_u0`, kinked forward below `door_kink_v`), the rear haunch plates and the engine cover. So the nose reads as two green fender plates round a dark, stroked bonnet with the green pentagon in it, and the front carries as much green as the tail. An orange-and-black hazard band (`patterns.hazard`, top `hazard_v` 0.20, period 44 px, duty 0.45) runs arch to arch (`hazard_u0` 0.12 to the rear arch) over the plates' feet and the sill strips, under a dark lower body; the sill parts (`sill_l`, `sill_r`) carry a whole-part hazard under it for the texels no view maps.
- **10 m:**
  - **Tones:** dark violet on the lower body ahead of the door plate (below `skirt_v` 0.40), the segment between the door and haunch plates, the bonnet's outer facets, the fender behind its raked end, the bumper corners and grille uprights, the bonnet pins (`bonnet_pins`, also on the engine cover), the roof, the buttresses behind `deck_u0`, the wing, the endplates and the rear valance.
  - **Strokes:** `geom.outline` everywhere, as wide in the texture on every island: each green plate a near-black `keyline` 16 px then an orange `pin` 14 px (the fender's raked end included); the bonnet's own outline where it meets the fenders, inside the bonnet, keyline then pin (`_bonnet_seam`: the outline's parts inside u `seam_u0` -0.05 to `seam_u1` 0.3 and beyond |w| `seam_w` 0.2, carried into the texture through the bonnet's top map), so green fender, near-black, orange, dark bonnet; the skirt an orange pin; the engine cover ringed inside its outer edge near-black `rim_key` 24, orange `rim_px` 24, near-black 16 (holes unstroked); the roof chevrons keyline and pin.
  - **Over the top:** where the side skin (`side_*_skin`) faces up and the top view maps it (the haunch shoulder, the top of the dark segment), the dark segment, the door plate, the haunch plate and the orange line carry on across it from where they leave the side (`_onto_top`).
  - **Band end:** behind the fender's raked end, between the front arch and the number plate, `dash_n` 5 thin solid orange slashes (`dash_tone` 1; `dash_w` 0.009, `dash_gap` 0.014 from `dash_u0` 0.31), leaning with the rake, their tops on `dash_v1` 0.78 under the window and each foot `dash_rise` 0.05 higher than the last from `dash_v0` 0.46 (the first stands on the skirt's pin), so each is shorter towards the tail and the last two end above the plate's frame: the reference's beltline dashes. Solid orange, not stroked green: they are orange lines like the one behind the door plate, so no green meets violet unstroked.
  - **Front:** each bumper corner carries a hazard band along its foot (`bump_haz` 50 px tall in its texture, slashes at `bump_lean` -30, which lean with the side band's rake on both sides), joining the splitter's hazard and the side band, so the front carries the tail's hazard at bumper height; above it a green `trim_px` 18 px trim `trim_gap` 14 px inside the rest of the corner's outline traces the intake and runs along the band's top. The grille opening's side walls (`grille_side_l`, `grille_side_r`) and the grille uprights carry the same hazard (`grille_tone` 2, `upright_haz` 1), seen through the mesh, so the hazard runs across the whole nose at bumper height. The pentagon's green echo outline is off (`echo` 0): its point ran off the bonnet's front edge in loose green ticks.
  - **Features:** orange mirrors, the fender's arch vent (`fender_arch_vent`) green with the fender, dark endplates, near-black intake strakes (`strake_tone` 2: violet ones stuck out of the silhouette beyond the green fender as loose violet teeth in front34; near-black they read as canards and, from the side, louvres), near-black swan necks, and a keylined and pinned green stripe along both wing faces (`wing_h` 0.13 of the chord, in the texture).
  - Every slash and cut leans `lean_deg` 25 (top towards the tail); the sill hazard leans the same way per side, the splitter and rear face at 30.
- **1 m:**
  - **Hairline** (`hair` 10 px): dark green inside the door plate.
  - **Side type** (`typeset.type_block`, one line per block so each side sets from the nose-side edge), sheared to the lean, on the vertical side face: a green 01 (`unit_size` 0.038) outlined 14 px in orange, its raked front edge at `unit_u` 0.677 (at v 0.55), top at `unit_v` 0.66, and TYPE-01 in orange under it `unit_gap` 0.075 below, both ahead of the sim's door light panel; the orange line behind the door plate ends in a point at `line_v0` 0.75, above the 01. On the door plate EVA-01 (`tag_v` 0.76) over a 10 px near-black rule, NERV and TEST TYPE in dark violet under it, from the plate's front edge plus `tag_margin` 0.012.
  - **Top:** an outlined 01 on the roof reading from the front (green pin, near-black keyline), EVA-01 on the engine cover reading from behind (`cover_tag_u` 0.796), and ahead of it on the cover `chev_n` 2 near-black chevrons pointing to the tail (`chev_point` 1, apexes from `chev_u0` 0.746, `chev_step` 0.024, `chev_t` 0.014, arms `chev_arm` 0.025 over `chev_w` 0.1), wholly behind the cover's scoop slot (`engine_cover_scoop`, painted green with the plate).
  - **Numbers:** near-black plates with an orange frame round the kit's number boxes on `door_l`, `door_r` and `rear_face`; the door plates carry a near-black keyline round the frame, the tail's doesn't (it runs along the bumper edge).

## Constraints that shaped the layout

- **Door light panel** (`door_light_panel_l/_r`, keep_clear, u 0.712-0.772, v 0.45-0.70): text may not lie under it. It fills the dark segment between the door and haunch plates at v 0.45-0.70, so the 01 sits ahead of it in the 0.05 u between the door plate's pin (about u 0.66 at v 0.55) and u 0.712, which caps the 01 at 0.038; TYPE-01 can't sit over the 01 there and goes under it, above the intake crease (v 0.34 at u 0.70). The 01's outline still lies partly under the panel (a warning: the sim covers it).
- **min_outline_px 14:** the approved 9 px pins are refused, so every pin is 14 px. A green slash with a 14 px stroke each side is about 46 px wide, so stroked green slashes come out as orange-framed boxes with a speck of green.
- **Slash run:** above the number plate only about v 0.68-0.79 is free (the plate's frame reaches v 0.68: box top v 0.53 plus 42 px at about 282 px per unit v; the window line is at v 0.826 at u 0.38, and `near_tangent_px` 12 keeps tops under it). That is about 30 texture px, too short for a 25° lean to show (12 px of shift), so stroked slashes there read as upright boxes ("ΠΠΠ", round 11). So the run sits ahead of the plate, in the free door area between the front arch (`front_arch_l` rear edge u 0.284 at v 0.6, 0.26 at v 0.7) and the plate's front (u 0.38), where it is up to v 0.32 tall, and its slashes are solid and thin. The first slashes cross the door's front edge (`door_front_edge_*`) at about 45°.
- **Grille:** behind the mesh, the opening's floor and centre (screen pixels of front34 between the uprights) have no texture map; the paint that shows there is the side walls `grille_side_l` (i138) and `grille_side_r` (i070), and the uprights (i147, i069). `crash_opening` (i172) shows nothing from front34 and also holds the wheel-arch liners, so it stays unpainted.
- **Engine cover scoop:** the slot on the cover's centreline (`engine_cover_scoop`, i114) is about u 0.68-0.69 and |w| up to about 0.1 on top, and the cover chevrons don't paint across it. Ahead of it there is only u 0.627-0.68 inside the rim, so the chevrons go behind it, between u 0.70 and EVA-01 (u 0.78-0.81), which caps them at 2 with short arms.
- **Bumper corner hazard:** the corners are mapped by no view, so the band is the bottom `bump_haz` px of each island's texture (i020 x 1024-1284, i021 x 1609-2017, foot at y 407), the hazard drawn on the whole part and the dark painted back over the rest (`patterns.hazard` takes only whole parts without a view).
- **Fender nose maps:** the side maps of i120 (`fender_nose_l`) and i086 (`fender_nose_r`) hold only v ≤ 0.565 at u 0, 0.66 at u 0.03, 0.725 at u 0.06, 0.745 at u 0.09 (in side car space i086 is a thin diagonal strip from v 0.45 at u -0.01 to v 0.70 at u 0.12, over the headlight); the crest beyond is mapped from the top view only. Any side-view edge on the fender that reaches the crest stops at the map's measured edge in texel steps (the stepped notches and orange shards of rounds 8-9). So the fender is green as whole islands, its only side-view edge the raked end over the arch (on i072/i123, side-mapped), and its top edge the bonnet seam, stroked from the bonnet's side.
- **Bonnet seam stroke:** no primitive strokes the split between two islands that no single view maps both sides of, so `_bonnet_seam` strokes `bonnet_skin`'s (i099) own texture outline (`geom.outline`, `view=None`, inside, outer rings) and selects the fender-side parts with car-space boxes mapped through i099's top map (`along`). The bonnet has a notch at its front (no map at u 0.0-0.012, |w| 0.096-0.132, by the bonnet pins); `seam_w` under 0.2 strokes round it as short orange hooks beside the pentagon's point, and a box across the front edge (u < 0.03) strokes it as orange spears converging on the point; so the front edge between |w| 0.13 and 0.2 (u about 0) stays unstroked, a short dark-to-green edge at the nose tip.
- **Fender rear end:** the fender's rear edge (`fender_rear_edge_*`) runs u 0.285 at v 0.69 to 0.279 at v 0.8; a raked end through `blade_u1` 0.29 (as before) lies behind it above v 0.6 and is clipped off, leaving green against the door with no stroke; 0.22 keeps the whole raked end and its stroke on the fender skin.
- **Bumper hazard:** the side band reaches the bumper corner below `hazard_v` 0.20 when `hazard_u0` is under 0.12, but the corner's side-mapped area there is a sliver (wedged -0.06..0.12: a few orange bits on the grille upright and the lip), so the band stays arch to arch.
- **Engine cover:** the roof/cover shut line runs about 10 px inside the cover's front edge (texture 1100, 1205); an outer keyline under 24 px runs along it.
- **Wing stripe:** a shut line runs along the top of wing_top (texture y 168); `wing_h` above 0.13 puts the orange edge within 12 px of it. The stripe is texture boxes: the wing isn't laid out along the top view's u, and `geom.outline` without a view strokes only a part's own outline.
- **Bumper trim:** the corners (i020, i021) are mapped by no view, so the trim is a ring inset in their texture outlines above the hazard band (`geom.outline` without a view strokes only a whole part's outline, not the part less the band); its wobble on the car is theirs. The 14 px dark spacer is the corner's own colour and isn't drawn.
- **Sills:** the sill strips are only partly mapped; side plates skip them (`_pieces`), the hazard band covers what's mapped and the whole-part hazard under it the rest.
- **Haunch type:** type on the haunch wraps onto the top and shows upside down from above, so the 01 group stays on the vertical face.

## Known weaknesses

- **Nose tip:** the bonnet's front edge between |w| 0.13 and 0.2 is unstroked (above), and the fender's edge along the headlight top (the thin i086/i120 strip) meets the dark bumper corner unstroked.
- **Slash step at the door's front edge (kit):** in sim 20261020-225706 the first slash, straight in car space, steps about 5 screen px towards the nose where it crosses from `door_l` (i128) onto `fender_rear_l` (i158), at about u 0.33, v 0.56 (side_l); on the right, `door_r` (i046) to `fender_rear_r` (i054), about 2 px (side_r). Reported as a kit fault, not designed around.
- **Two chevron directions:** the roof's chevrons point to the nose, the cover's to the tail (as on the reference's rear deck).
- **Not seen in the shots:** the presets have no straight front shot; the bonnet vent and the rear bodywork over the tail lights are plain.
