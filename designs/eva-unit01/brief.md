## The brief

The car as Unit-01's armour: a deep violet car with acid-green armour plates laid on whole body panels, the way Unit-01's green sits on its shoulder pylons, chest and jaw, and Unit-01's orange as hard lines where plates meet. From his reference, a Unit-01 Porsche by another painter: its density and angular energy (green lines that cut across the panels like armour seams, a low hazard band, chevrons, tactical markings all over), not its exact layout.

- **50 m:** violet with green armour plates (the front fenders, the bonnet centre, a lightning-cut door plate, the rear haunches, the engine cover) over a dark lower body, and an orange-and-black hazard band of steady height along the lower body, arch to arch, under the plates.
- **10 m:** a darker violet in large facets (the lower body, the segment between the door and haunch plates, the bonnet's outer corners, the bumper sides, roof, rear deck, wing). Every green-to-violet edge is stroked the same way, near-black then orange, wide enough to read at 10 m. Behind the fender's raked end, a run of thin raked orange slashes, each shorter, climbs the door ahead of the number (the reference's beltline dashes); an orange pinline along the dark lower body; the hazard carried across the nose, through the grille; the car's features in the accent; every slash leans one way. The front carries a second layer as dense as the tail's.
- **1 m:** tone-on-tone hairlines, and the side type as a designed element: a big raked 01 outlined in orange with TYPE-01 set under it on the same raked edge, clear of the sim's door light panel, and on the door plate a raked EVA-01 over a rule with NERV and TEST TYPE under it, all leaning with the rake, set from the plate's front edge, wholly on the vertical side face. Small markings elsewhere: an outlined 01 on the roof, EVA-01 on the engine cover.

Constraints: violet, green and orange, with near-black; the number zones stay clear and readable (the number is white, and violet gives it contrast); edges clearly cross or avoid seams and shut lines. No traced or redrawn Evangelion or NERV logos: the inspiration is the palette and the armour, not marks.
