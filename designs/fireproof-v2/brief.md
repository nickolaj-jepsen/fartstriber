## The brief

The same flame car, now burning: v1's orange car with the yellow core band on the sides and the yellow spine on top, edged in black, burns into a black tail along a raked line, and the line is a pixel dissolve in the pixel flame's own language. The front burns too, lighter, so both ends tell one story. From the references: a colour change told as a pixel or triangle dissolve (Paul Miller BMW, Trilateral Grey), a front colour with a black tail, raked splits and small sponsors (Getspeed), a black lower body with hazard chevrons (WRT).

- **50 m:** an orange front and a black tail wedge on a steep rake, the yellow band nose to tail, a yellow spine from the lip over the roof to the engine cover. No big wordmark.
- **10 m:** the dissolve in square pixels of three sizes, densest at the line: black eats into the orange ahead of it, orange and yellow embers fly back over the black. The front breaks up the same way behind the front arch and back over the bonnet. Flame tongues in the mark's lighter orange rise from the black sill, and hairlines of the same tone rake with the burn. The car's features in yellow; a black nose plate with the spine running through it; hazard hatching on the splitter lip.
- **1 m:** charcoal ash pixels in the black; small roster sponsors on the black sills and haunches, the mirrors and the bonnet; at most one small, discreet "fireproof".

Constraints: every number zone stays on one solid field with contrast for the white 451; orange, flame orange, yellow, black and ash (sponsors may add the proof cream); edges clearly cross or avoid seams and shut lines.
