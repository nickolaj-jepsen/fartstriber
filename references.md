# References

The owner's favourite liveries, the taste anchors, one line each on what makes them work. They mark each yes or no in `livery review --references`. `livery references fetch` keeps the yes rows' showroom pictures in `.cache/references/<#>/`, never committed. A brief names the two or three rows it draws on (`References: 25, 32`), and builders and the critic look at those pictures.
